Honest criticism is welcome; personal attacks are not

We welcome thoughtful questions, constructive criticism, and respectful disagreement about marketing strategies, tools, and our own content. Debate the idea as sharply as you like, but never attack other learners, commenters, or instructors personally.

Open moderation and blocking

We practice open moderation so genuine questions and critiques stay visible. However, repeat offenders who harass other participants or flood discussions with spam will be blocked from commenting.

What we remove without notice

To keep our learning environment useful and safe, moderators remove comments that contain:

  • Defamation, personal harassment, or threats against any individual.
  • Bigotry, hate speech, racism, casteism, sexism, or slurs of any kind.
  • Profanity, obscene language, or sexually explicit material.
  • Self-promotional spam, unauthorized affiliate links, or unrelated commercial pitches.
  • Keyword-stuffed display names or link-bait handles (please use your real first name or a normal handle, not an SEO phrase).

Questions about moderation

If you believe a genuine comment or question was removed in error, reach out to us at admin@kicoedtech.xyz.
