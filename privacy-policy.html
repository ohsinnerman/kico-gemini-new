How the enquiry form works on this website

The course enquiry form on this website stores nothing on our web servers or in any site database. When you fill in your name, phone number, email address, city, state, gender, or preferred start date and press submit, your browser formats those details into a message and opens WhatsApp (or your default email client) on your own device. KiCo receives your details only when you choose to send that WhatsApp message or email.

Personal information we collect

You can browse our website without revealing your identity. We collect personal identification information (such as your name, email address, postal address, or phone number) only when you voluntarily share it with us, for example when you contact us on WhatsApp, send us an email, or enrol in a batch. Refusing to share personal details is always your right, though it may prevent us from enrolling you in a course or replying to an enquiry.

Non-personal technical information and cookies

When you visit our site, standard non-personal technical data may be logged by your browser or hosting provider, including your browser type, operating system, device category, and internet service provider.

Our website or third-party services may also use browser cookies for basic record-keeping and aggregate traffic measurement. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies or alert you before a cookie is saved. Some interactive features on external services may not function as expected if cookies are disabled.

How we use your information

KiCo uses the information you voluntarily provide to:

  • Personalise your learning experience and understand how visitors use our training resources as a group.
  • Improve our curriculum, website clarity, and student support based on your feedback.
  • Respond directly to your course enquiries, admissions questions, and support requests.
  • Send administrative emails or messages replying to your specific questions and batch updates.

How we protect and share information

We apply appropriate administrative and technical security practices to guard against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of personal and enrolment records.

We never sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to anyone. We may share generic, aggregated demographic statistics that do not identify any individual with trusted education partners or affiliates.

Advertising partners and Google cookies

Advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies (such as the Google DART cookie) to display relevant advertisements based on visits to this and other websites. These cookies use non-personally identifiable information and do not track your name, email address, or physical address. You can opt out of personalised Google advertising at any time through your Google Ad Settings.

Policy updates and how to contact us

KiCo may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes take effect when posted on this page, and continued use of the website signifies your acceptance of the updated policy.
