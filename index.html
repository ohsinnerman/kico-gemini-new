Foundations, Strategy & Digital Workspace

Weeks 1–2 · 5 modules

Core Marketing Concepts

Understand customer needs, wants, and demand alongside the 4Ps marketing mix, segmentation, targeting, and positioning. Learn how brands create value, earn customer loyalty, and shape consumer decision-making.

Digital Marketing Fundamentals

Master the core principles and policies of digital marketing, run a complete brand audit, and define a clear brand persona. Build structured career and marketing plans backed by real-world case studies.

Inbound Marketing

Compare inbound and outbound methodology, attract visitors with useful content and search visibility, and convert traffic into leads. Nurture prospects with email automation and measure performance against clear KPIs.

Google Workspace

Collaborate in real time across Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Drive while managing schedules and communication in Calendar, Gmail, and Meet. Apply workflow automation and productivity integrations used by modern marketing teams.

Domain, Hosting & Website Setup

Distinguish between domains, hosting types, and site builders so you can register a brand-aligned domain and choose reliable hosting. Connect your domain to a CMS and configure SSL certificates, security, and baseline site settings.

Websites, WordPress, Search & Local Visibility

Weeks 3–5 · 6 modules

Website Planning & Creation

Define clear website goals, select the right platform, and map an intuitive site architecture. Design responsive, mobile-friendly user experiences backed by solid domain, hosting, and security fundamentals.

WordPress

Set up a complete WordPress site, customize themes to match your brand identity, and install essential plugins for SEO, speed, and security. Publish and manage posts, pages, menus, and media while handling backups and updates.

Landing Page Optimization

Learn how high-performing landing pages drive conversions through persuasive copy and clear, mobile-first layouts. Diagnose and fix the common structural mistakes that cause visitors to bounce before acting.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

Understand how search engines crawl, index, and rank pages using modern algorithms. Execute keyword research, on-page optimization, and off-page authority building through backlinks and brand mentions.

Content Marketing

Build trust and authority using blogs, videos, infographics, case studies, and social posts aligned with audience intent. Plan distribution channels, measure content ROI, and repurpose assets across platforms.

Local Promotion

Optimize Google Business Profile listings, location-based keywords, and map rankings to capture nearby customers. Combine local ads, WhatsApp engagement, and neighbourhood partnerships or events to drive footfall and enquiries.

Social Media, Video, Design & Community Channels

Weeks 6–8 · 7 modules

Social Media Marketing

Select the right social platforms for each business goal, produce engaging organic content, and grow active communities. Plan and launch paid social campaigns for targeted reach and measurable engagement.

Facebook Marketing

Set up and optimize a Facebook Business Page across posts, stories, reels, and live sessions. Run targeted campaigns in Meta Ads Manager, install the Meta Pixel for conversion tracking and remarketing, and read Insights reports.

Instagram Marketing

Optimize your Instagram profile, use reels, hashtags, and trends for organic reach, and collaborate with creators on UGC campaigns. Launch targeted Instagram ads in Meta Ads Manager and track growth with Instagram Insights.

LinkedIn Marketing

Position personal profiles and company pages for B2B credibility, publish thought-leadership articles, and expand your industry network. Run LinkedIn Ads for B2B lead generation and refine outreach using LinkedIn Analytics.

Video Marketing

Use storytelling frameworks to script and produce memorable marketing videos across formats. Distribute video assets organically, run paid video ad campaigns for conversions, and evaluate retention and view metrics.

Quora Marketing

Build a credible Quora profile, identify high-traffic questions in your niche, and write value-driven storytelling answers that attract qualified readers. Launch Quora Ads and track funnel performance in Quora Analytics.

Graphic Designing

Apply core visual design principles and tools to create social posts, ad banners, thumbnails, logos, and cohesive brand kits. Export and optimize crisp graphics tailored for web, mobile feeds, and print campaigns.

Lead Generation, E-Commerce, Mobile & Monetisation

Weeks 9–10 · 6 modules

Online Lead Generation

Design compelling lead magnets, high-converting forms, and landing pages that capture qualified prospects through organic and paid channels. Track, nurture, and close leads using CRM pipelines and marketing automation.

Email Marketing

Build and segment subscriber lists, write high-open subject lines and persuasive copy, and design mobile-friendly email templates. Set up automated drip sequences and monitor open rates, CTR, conversions, and unsubscribes.

E-Commerce Marketing

Set up an online store, optimize product pages, and drive qualified shoppers via SEO, Google Ads, and paid social. Recover abandoned carts with email and remarketing, compare marketplace vs. owned-store strategies, and track ROI.

App Store Optimization (ASO) & Mobile Marketing

Optimize app titles, descriptions, keywords, icons, and screenshots while earning ratings and reviews to lift store rankings. Run paid app-install campaigns across Google Play, the App Store, and social platforms.

Google AdSense

Meet AdSense eligibility requirements, configure your account, and place ad units strategically to grow publisher revenue without hurting user experience. Optimize CPC, CPM, and CTR using AdSense reports and Google Analytics.

Digital Payment System

Integrate modern payment gateways and methods protected by encryption, OTP verification, and fraud prevention. Understand how fast, transparent digital payments - including contactless, BNPL, and emerging rails - improve checkout conversion.

Analytics, Reputation, Client Reporting & Internship

Weeks 11–12 · 5 modules

Web Analytics

Set up Google Analytics (GA4) to track essential traffic, engagement, and conversion metrics across web properties. Interpret user behaviour flows and build clear dashboards and reports to guide marketing decisions.

Online Reputation Management

Monitor brand mentions, search results, and customer reviews to protect and strengthen a business's public image. Respond professionally to negative feedback, encourage authentic reviews, and use PR and content to build credibility.

Digital Marketing Tools

Build a practical toolkit spanning keyword and SEO research, social scheduling, email automation, analytics tracking, and paid ad management so you can execute and scale campaigns efficiently.

Reports & Proposals

Structure persuasive client proposals and clear campaign performance reports using charts, graphs, and visual dashboards. Communicate ROI, scope, and recommendations with confidence to employers or freelance clients.

Internship & Live Projects

Apply all 29 modules to real client briefs and live marketing campaigns while developing teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills. Graduate with documented case studies and project reports in your portfolio.
